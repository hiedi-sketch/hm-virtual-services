Privacy Policy — HM Virtual Services

Last Updated: May 29, 2026

1. Introduction HM Virtual Services ("we," "us," or "our") operates a bookkeeping and virtual assistant management platform ("the App") accessible at [yourapp.replit.app]. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our App.

2. Information We Collect

Account Information





Name and email address



Password (encrypted, never stored in plain text)

Business & Financial Information





Transaction data imported from QuickBooks Online for bookkeeping purposes



Receipts and supporting documents you or your bookkeeper uploads



Notes and responses related to transaction review

Payment Information





Invoices for services are processed through Square. We do not store your payment card details. Square's privacy policy applies to all payment processing. See square.com/legal/privacy

Usage Information





Login timestamps



Actions taken within the App (for support and security purposes)

3. How We Use Your Information We use collected information to:





Provide bookkeeping and virtual assistant services



Facilitate transaction review and client communication



Process service invoices via Square



Respond to your questions and support requests



Improve the App's functionality

4. How We Share Your Information We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with:





Intuit/QuickBooks — to read and write transaction data on your behalf



Square — to process service invoice payments



Asana / ClickUp — if you have selected either as your preferred communication channel, task data is shared with that platform



Law enforcement — if required by law

8. Your Rights You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us at hiedi@hmvirtualservices.com.

9. Cookies The App may use session cookies to maintain your logged-in state. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

10. Changes to This Policy We may update this policy periodically. We will notify active users of material changes via email.
