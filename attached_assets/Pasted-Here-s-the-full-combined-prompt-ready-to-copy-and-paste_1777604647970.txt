---

I have an existing client dashboard with a "Client Hub" section. I need you to make the following changes:

**1. Modify the existing "Add New Client" button**
Keep the existing "Add New Client" button at the top of the Client Hub. This button should continue to do what it already does — add a client quickly without any workflow.

**2. Add a new "Onboard a New Client" button**
Place a second button directly next to (or near) the existing "Add New Client" button at the top of the Client Hub. Label it **"Onboard a New Client"**.

When clicked, this button should launch a **multi-step onboarding workflow** that collects the following information about my new client:

**Step 1 — Service Selection**
Ask me to select which service(s) this client needs:
- Bookkeeping only
- Virtual Assistant (VA) only
- Both Bookkeeping & VA

The rest of the form should show only the sections relevant to the selected service(s).

**Step 2 — Business Basics (all clients)**
- Client full name
- Business name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Industry / type of business

**Step 3 — Bookkeeping Details** *(show only if Bookkeeping or Both was selected)*
- Accounting software they currently use (QuickBooks, Wave, Xero, FreshBooks, None, Other)
- Do they have existing accounts set up? (Yes / No)
- Fiscal year end (month)
- Are they on cash or accrual basis?
- Do they have a dedicated business bank account? (Yes / No)
- Any notes on their current bookkeeping situation

**Step 4 — Virtual Assistant Details** *(show only if VA or Both was selected)*
- What types of tasks do they need help with? (multi-select: email management, scheduling, data entry, customer follow-up, social media, research, other)
- Tools/platforms they use (e.g. Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Trello, Asana, etc.)
- Preferred communication method (email, Slack, Voxer, text, phone)
- Availability expectations / working hours

**Step 5 — Access & Credentials**
- A checklist or text field for noting what software access and logins the client will need to share with me separately — do NOT collect actual passwords in this form

**Step 6 — Goals & Priorities**
- What are their top 3 goals for working together?
- Anything else they want me to know?

**Step 7 — Client Portal Access Setup**
- Prompt the client to create a login for their own client portal
- Fields:
  - Email address (pre-filled from Step 2, but editable)
  - Create a password
  - Confirm password
- Password requirements: minimum 8 characters, at least one number
- Validate that both password fields match before allowing submission
- On submit, save these credentials to the existing user/auth system so the client can log into their own portal

**Step 8 — Confirmation & Submission**
- Show a summary of everything entered
- On submit, send the full onboarding details to my email using a mailto: link (I will configure the email address)
- Also save the client to my existing client list/database just like the "Add New Client" button does
- Display a success confirmation message that tells the client their portal is ready and includes their login email

**Design requirements:**
- The workflow should appear as a **modal or slide-in panel** (not a separate page) so I stay in my dashboard
- Show a **progress bar or step indicator** at the top so I can see where I am in the workflow
- Each step should have Back and Next buttons; the final step has a Submit button
- Match the existing dashboard's fonts, colors, and button styles exactly — do not introduce new design styles
- The form should be clean, readable, and work on both desktop and mobile
